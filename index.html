About

I sit at the point where operations meet data. Day to day, that means vetting supply movement at every stage of a food business — starting in the back office with budget and pricing, through procurement, production, and out to retail — and making sure nothing goes unaccounted for.

Increasingly, it also means replacing the manual side of that job with something that runs itself: spreadsheets that sync automatically across outlets, dashboards that surface variance before it becomes a problem, and — most recently — a proper database behind the whole operation instead of a stack of linked sheets.

Alongside that, I'm building MERN Stack development skills and taking on independent projects, with the aim of doing this kind of systems work for teams beyond my own.

Google Apps Script system syncing inventory and stock data across seven outlets — fuzzy name matching, alias mapping, and monthly consolidation, all built to route around Sheets' API limits. Extended the same Apps Script approach this year into a supply log and task-tracking system, plus daily EPOS sales data analysis.

Project details
Built on Google Apps Script and IMPORTRANGE, with fuzzy name-matching (Levenshtein distance) and an alias map to reconcile inconsistent product names across outlet sheets. Sheets' 50-call-per-spreadsheet limit meant a two-step aggregation pattern was needed — outlet-level summary tabs first, then a separate master consolidation pass. Now being migrated to a Postgres/Supabase backend fed directly by the EPOS API. This year's breakthrough extended the same automation approach into a dedicated supply log, a cross-team task-tracking system, and daily analysis of EPOS sales data.

Inventory accountability & loss recovery CONTROL

A compliance system that tracks every item entering the business to a destination, and a recovery process for anything that can't be accounted for — recovered through remittance into the expense account or salary deduction.

Project details
Each unaccounted item is logged in a loss register with its severity (high, medium or low), cost, the recovery action taken, and the corrective measure put in place so it doesn't recur. Recovery is immediate: the value is either remitted into the expense account or deducted from the responsible staff member's salary, so shortfalls are closed out rather than carried forward.
3severity tiers tracked
Raw material BOM reconciliation IN PROGRESS

A Postgres and Supabase system tracking shared raw materials across recipes, modeling a six-stage variance chain from back-office budgeting through to retail sales.

Project details
Schema includes an append-only stock-movement ledger and production-batch records that auto-explode a finished product into its raw-material draws via its bill of materials — built to solve reconciliation for shared ingredients (like flour) used across multiple recipes. Checks variance across six stages, starting in the back office before goods ever reach a retail outlet: (1) budget — approved budget vs. actual spend, budget deficit, price variance, and quantity variance; (2) procurement — expected vs. actual quantity procured; (3) issue to production; (4) production — actual output vs. expected output based on SOP-standardized production quantities; (5) supply to SCO — quantity supplied vs. quantity received; (6) retail outlets — quantity supplied vs. quantity received.
40products modeled
